## 1. Abstract / Key Concept
Presents Mem0, a developer-friendly memory layer that extracts facts and entities from conversations to maintain persistent state across sessions.

---

## 2. Core Mechanism & Methodology
Heuristic entity extraction via LLM prompts + flat key-value vector storage. Focuses solely on write throughput.

## 5. Limitations & Vulnerabilities
