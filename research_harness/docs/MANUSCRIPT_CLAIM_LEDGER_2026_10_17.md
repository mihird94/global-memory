---

## 1. Classification Taxonomy

1. **`Implemented`**: Code and schemas exist and are executed in the checked-in repositories (`global-memory` or `ccmp_recovery`).
2. **`Observed Result`**: Empirical outcome measured and archived in a durable, checksummed report or test output.
3. **`Conditional Model`**: Mathematical or parametric derivation whose validity depends on explicit preconditions.
4. **`Hypothesis`**: Conceptual mechanism proposed to explain empirical patterns, pending controlled isolation experiments.
5. **`Proposed Extension`**: Architecture, policy arm, or benchmark protocol pre-registered for future iterations.
